# Jail release cinematic

The first arrival of a new character plays a 15-second Blender cinematic. The uploaded orange STB jail player, two runtime white police rigs, U-shaped jail, furnished courtyard, towers, animated gate/barrier and modular bridge are imported from existing GLBs. Nearby city buildings use the current compact-map placement export. The user-uploaded jail rig replaces the earlier bare-player escort actor; no new paid generation was submitted.

The editable, packed source is `art/cinematics/jail-release/jail-release.blend`. It includes three complete rigs with native walk/idle strips, baked foot contact, an escort route, world-space gate/barrier pivots and five cameras. `build-jail-intro.py` reconstructs it from the recorded runtime assets. The manifest records source SHA-256 hashes and camera timings. `encode-jail-intro.py` packages the render as H.264/AAC MP4 with game footsteps, gate sounds and a three-second welcome ending. The ending uses the approved game logo and WELCOME TO text, rendered from the editable Blender card with flat graphic lighting by `render-jail-welcome.py`. The version 2 video and poster live in `apps/client/static/assets/cinematics/` and load only when needed.

Press Enter the Block to watch; Skip Intro or Escape continues immediately. Playback errors offer a Continue button, and reduced-motion users can explicitly choose Play or Continue. Sound follows the game's volume/mute settings. The game renderer pauses behind the movie to reduce phone workload.

Online completion is stored per account in the existing persistent 3D character JSON. Only a fresh character in the protected release area, outside combat, receives the authority's handoff position beyond the real gate and barrier. Completion is idempotent, and cannot recall an established character from elsewhere in the world. Returning accounts are migrated to seen without changing their position, inventory, cash or structures. Respawns do not reset the flag. An interrupted intro remains pending on reconnect. Solo saves retain an explicit first-arrival flag; legacy device saves skip the intro without changing their saved progress.

FPS content version 3 requires clients to refresh for the updated first-arrival contract. Shared gameplay messages remain version 1. This is a rendered opening scene; the multiplayer city keeps running while it plays, with the arriving player inside the existing protected jail release area.

Ground contact is baked from evaluated skinned mesh vertices against surface samples taken before the actors are added. Helper shapes and coarse object bounds are excluded from height and sole measurements. `check-jail-intro.py` independently measures all three actors at multiple points, including the gate crossing, and verifies a 2 mm sole clearance.

`assets:jail-intro` rebuilds the scene, renders the film and flat welcome card, then encodes the movie. The shared duration is 15 seconds; the welcome card occupies seconds 12–15. Old first-arrival flags and saves are retained, so established characters are not reset or forced through the intro again.

# First-person inventory

Players carry twenty item stacks total. The five hand slots reference items in those twenty slots; they do not add capacity. Guns and harvest tools take one slot each, resources and bullets stack to 500, and grenades, C4 and rocket ammunition stack to five. The rocket launcher is a nonstacking gun. Each individual gun retains its own loaded magazine when moved, stored, dropped, picked up, reassigned or reloaded from a save.

Open the popup with I, Tab or the BAG button. It shows the current Meshy black stick figure as a rotatable paper doll and uses the approved item/weapon artwork. Select an item and a hand slot to bind it, or press 1–5 while that item is selected in the popup. Outside the popup, 1–5 or the five hand buttons equip their item. Press H, tap the active hand button again, or use EMPTY HANDS to holster. UNBIND removes a shortcut without removing the item. I, Escape or CLOSE dismiss the popup; Tab remains available for keyboard focus navigation inside it.

Drag items to move, merge or swap on desktop. On phones, select MOVE and then the destination; tapping an empty cell also moves the selected item. SPLIT moves half a stack into an empty carried slot. DROP puts a persistent stack on the nearby floor; E / USE picks it up if visible and reachable on the same floor. A partial pickup leaves the uncollected remainder on the ground. Up to 200 dropped stacks are saved, and dropping beyond that limit is refused rather than deleting an item.

Existing resource wealth is migrated once, without deleting the old base, crate history or gathered-resource state. The starter loadout contains the existing six gun types, axe, pickaxe, five grenades, five C4 and five rockets. Up to 2,000 wood and up to 500 of each existing ore are put in the carried inventory; the remaining resources go into the starter reserve. A fresh player therefore still owns the original 10,000 wood: 2,000 carried and 8,000 reserved. The reserve is accessible only within six metres of the town spawn at 0 E / 14 S, through STORE and the reserve withdrawal buttons. Supplies in reserve cannot pay remote building costs or refill carried explosives.

Gathering requires the appropriate carried axe/pickaxe and room for the complete strike reward. A full inventory does not damage or deplete that resource. Searching a crate also requires room for its reward and leaves the crate unsearched when full. Construction spends carried wood; dismantling requires space for its refund before it removes the structure. New explosives consume real carried charges at the existing launch/placement moment, including the delayed C4 range check. Grenades and C4 show their remaining item count rather than replenishing free charge magazines. Rocket firing consumes one carried rocket and retains the existing launcher reload animation. Regular guns now reload from their matching carried ammo: pistol, SMG, rifle, sniper or LMG rounds. The reload animation consumes only the rounds needed to fill the magazine when it completes; partial supplies produce a partial reload and cancellation spends nothing. Rockets remain charged at launch, so their reload does not consume another rocket.

The optional `inventory` field lives inside the current version-1 device-local prototype save. Its nested schema records carried stacks, five ID-based shortcuts, active hand slot, reserve stacks, individual magazine values, and ground drops. The legacy `wood`/resource counters reflect carried resources for existing construction/HUD code. Invalid optional inventory data does not wipe an otherwise valid base. The explicit local reset creates the starter inventory again; death preserves it. Resource/drop/build transfers persist immediately. Automatic gunfire updates magazine state immediately and batches device writes over 200 ms, flushing when paused, opening inventory or leaving the page.

Opening the inventory pauses the single-player world, clears held movement/fire/look input, cancels pending actions, releases mouse capture and focuses the dialog. The paper doll renders at 20 fps only while open. Closing it releases its preview context and resumes the prior play state. Shared player geometry/materials are retained for the game. The previous online game/database remain separate.

Verification covers capacity, all stack limits, conservation through split/merge/transfer/drop/pickup, five shortcut references, per-gun magazines, finite charges, migration and save validation. Browser checks use actual desktop/touch controls, resource harvesting, dropped-item pickup, and repeated popup open/close rather than writable game-state hooks.

The ammo-model update grants starter bullets once: 120 pistol, 180 SMG, 180 rifle, 30 sniper and 240 LMG rounds. Available carried slots receive them; overflow goes to the existing spawn reserve. The `ammoVersion: 1` migration marker prevents fresh grants on reload or death. Existing weapons, magazines, buildings, loot history and supplies remain intact. Previously searched crates remain searched. Unsearched containers now award their existing wood reward plus one deterministic ammo type (24 pistol / 30 SMG / 30 rifle / 5 sniper / 45 LMG rounds, cycling by container index). Both rewards must fit before either is awarded or the container is marked searched.

Six Blender ammo models and their transparent rendered icons are used in inventory, reserve, hotbar, held items and ground drops. Five bullet-only models provide cosmetic player projectiles and enemy bullets; the complete Blender rocket replaces the old primitive rocket projectile. Player firearm damage remains the existing hitscan system. Cosmetic player bullets are bounded to 32 concurrent visuals and do not apply a second hit. Download editable models and textures from `/assets/ammo/street-survival-ammo.zip`.

## Player-built storage

Approach a placed storage chest within 3.2 m on the same floor, with a clear path to it, and press **E** or tap **USE**. Its popup shows your 20 carried slots and the chest's 20 slots. Select an item on either side, choose a full stack, half stack or one item, then **DEPOSIT** or **WITHDRAW**. Transfers accept only what fits and leave the rest at the source. Guns retain their magazine counts; depositing an equipped item clears its hand-slot binding.

Each placed chest saves its contents independently on this device. Existing chests start empty when first opened. A chest containing items cannot be dismantled; withdraw its contents first. Chest capacity uses the same stack limits as the bag: resources and bullets 500, explosives 5, guns/tools 1. Escape or CLOSE returns to the game.

# Player structure durability

Every placed piece has a material and health. Wood has 100 HP, stone 300 HP, and metal 500 HP. One C4 charge deals exactly 100 structural damage: a full-health piece takes one, three, or five charges respectively. This applies to foundations, floors, walls, doorways, doors, and player storage. Structural damage is separate from the existing falloff used for character damage.

A charge's attached piece receives the full structural hit. Other pieces within 2.5 metres of the blast (measured to their actual surfaces) can receive the same hit if not shielded by solid cover. All hits are determined against the cover present at the beginning of that explosion. Pieces at zero health are removed from saved structures, visible batches, collision, and bullet cover. Damaged pieces remain usable. Intact pieces retain their elevations if an old implicit foundation is destroyed; this change does not add a separate structural-collapse simulation. Permanent map buildings and props remain outside the player-structure damage system. Guns, grenades, and rockets retain their prior behavior against structures.

The Build panel offers Wood, Stone, and Metal; T cycles material on desktop. New pieces receive the selected tier and full health. Stone and metal have distinct simple material visuals using the existing modular footprints. During the wood-only practice economy, these tiers cost three and five times the original wood cost. Dismantling refunds the remaining-health fraction of that cost, so dismantling and replacing a damaged piece is not free repair. C4 destruction awards no refund.

Aiming at a player piece shows its material, current/max HP, and health meter. Existing saves remain version 1. Legacy pieces without material or health are treated as full-health wood, without rewriting their coordinates, wood balance, or searched-loot indices. Partial damage and new material choices survive reload; zero-health records are excluded on load. New optional metadata is sanitized independently to avoid resetting an otherwise valid old base.

`structure-health.ts` defines tier stats; `structure-damage.ts` matches physical piece parts (including open doors), applies damage, and removes destroyed records. The existing C4 path records the hit piece ID, persists changed structures, and rebuilds its existing rendering/cover batches. `tests/structure-health.test.ts` checks all piece kinds against the exact charge counts, save compatibility, damage persistence, cover/range, heights, refunds, and removed collision. `tests/browser/structure-health.py` exercises real C4 input, saved legacy wood destruction, stone damage, health UI, tier selection, and reload persistence in desktop and touch browser contexts.

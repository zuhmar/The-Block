# Building and prop references

Original AI-generated concept sheets for the five buildings currently in `apps/client/src/fps/building-layout.ts`. Each PNG includes an exterior, a roof-removed interior study, and six separate furniture/utility prop studies in a coordinated glossy cartoon style.

- General Store: checkout/register, retail shelf, drinks refrigerator, storage cabinet, trash bin, wall vent.
- The Haven: single bed, bedside cabinet, waiting bench, dining table/stools, kitchenette, reception desk.
- Workshop: workbench/vise, tool chest, pegboard, parts cabinet, fabrication table, exhaust fan.
- Old Residence: sofa, coffee table, TV/media cabinet, double bed, wardrobe, kitchenette.
- Warehouse: shelving rack, pallet, carton stack, packing bench, cabinet, pallet jack.

These are concept references for review and later Blender work. They do not replace the current models or change gameplay, collisions, doors, fixtures, or saved data. Generated details and interiors are design studies, not measured floor plans; implementation must respect navigable circulation and existing openings. Branding variations on the sheets are presentation decoration, not approved replacement logos. Individual game props should have geometry only, without painted terrain patches or baked ground effects.

Image dimensions and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `manifest.json`. Original generated files remain in `/workspace/generated_images`.

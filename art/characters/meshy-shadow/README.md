# Animated Meshy shadow figure

The uploaded `original.glb` is preserved byte-for-byte (SHA-256 in `report.json`). It has one skinned mesh, 32 bones and five supplied clips. `animated-character.blend` retains the full-detail character, packed original textures, all native curves and the new editable combat actions.

Run `pnpm assets:meshy-shadow` to rebuild from the preserved upload. The script authors FK keys on the existing Mixamo-named arm bones using a geometric two-bone solve. Combat poses have a two-handed guard/hold, recoil recovery, left/right hook wind-up and follow-through, and a support-hand reload reach/withdraw/insert/return. No replacement skeleton or character geometry is generated.

Added clips: `Punch_Left`, `Punch_Right`; and `Pistol`, `SMG`, `Rifle`, `Sniper` variants of `Hold`, `Fire` and `Reload` (14 new clips). The supplied `Running`, `Walking`, `Idle_3`, `Regular_Jump` and `CrouchLookAroundBow` remain available. Native curves are retimed to the source project's 30 FPS timebase; the runtime export is sampled at 30 FPS and may round a native clip by one frame.

The optimized runtime export is `apps/client/src/fps/models/meshy-shadow/animated.glb`: about 24k triangles, 1024px textures, one skin, all 19 clips. Decimation is applied before the armature modifier so deformation is not baked into the rest mesh. Embedded source textures are matched by their original byte hashes, not Blender-generated image names, preserving base-color/normal/roughness assignments. Full-detail geometry and textures remain in the Blender source.

The live player now uses this rig. The client preloads one optimized model, shares its geometry and textures across skeleton clones, and draws forearm/hand triangles separately from the headless, armless world body. The local player body uses native idle/walk/run/jump clips, with upper-body combat tracks layered independently. Walk/run transitions blend, and game physics supplies jump height. Crouch remains available in the viewer; there is no new crouch control.

The four Blender weapons mount to the right hand. Left-hand long-gun poses align under the receiver. Punch and reload clips are sampled from the existing combat timers, while firing clips respond to shot events. Weapon slides, magazines and muzzle flashes remain separate parts. A depth-cleared first-person pass prevents walls and the world body from hiding the hands. Muzzle positions are transformed back into world space for tracers. Movement, aim, damage, magazine counts, reload rules, construction and saved data retain their existing systems.

Use **View Player & Weapons → MESHY BASE → Animation** to inspect all 19 clips or download the animated GLB. Closing the viewer releases its own model and textures without disposing the active player's asset. If the player asset fails to load, the existing procedural player remains usable. The original upload remains untouched. Hand helper bones are retained, but the uploaded mitten mesh is not a fully articulated five-finger hand.

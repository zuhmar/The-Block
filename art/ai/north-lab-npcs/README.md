# North Works laboratory NPC sprites

Three original AI-generated white stick figure designs, matching the existing approved NPC character reference (`art/characters/npc-image-rebuild/reference.png`) and the game's glossy cartoon style.

- **Easy — Lab worker:** teal work coat, backward cap and raised goggles; slim, lightly equipped silhouette.
- **Medium — Lab security:** amber vest, charcoal uniform, open-face helmet and moderate protection.
- **Hard — Lab enforcer:** crimson heavy armor, larger shoulder/knee protection and helmet with a clear face shield.

All retain featureless spherical white heads and white hands. No weapons are included. `reference.png` is the coordinated source sheet; the three named PNGs are individual transparent sprites extracted at a common canvas size and baseline without changing their designs. Dimensions and alpha checks are recorded in `manifest.json`.

These files are artwork references/sprites only. They do not add new character models, animations, combat behavior or NPC spawns to the game. Existing NPCs are unchanged.
